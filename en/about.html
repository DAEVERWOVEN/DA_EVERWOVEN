DA UNIVERSE · THE ASHEN ATLAS

About DA UNIVERSE

DA UNIVERSE: The Ashen Atlas is a free, original interactive fan gamebook set in the Dragon Age universe. Its new story is grounded in close study of the games, official novels, comics and lore, without reproducing the games' scripts.

Create your path

Choose a human, elf, dwarf or qunari; class, origin and starting attributes. Your character traits can open different dialogue and choices. Each created character has their own saved playthrough.

The story comes first

Play through narrative scenes, investigate mysteries, gather testimony, meet allies and rivals. Branching choices, relationships and endings are shaped by your decisions.

Languages and availability

The Android app supports Ukrainian, English and Russian. This website is available in all three languages. A public APK will only be offered after a separate release.

Google sign-in and saves

The Android app uses Google Sign-In to identify the account and requests Google Drive appDataFolder access only to synchronize its own save file. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
