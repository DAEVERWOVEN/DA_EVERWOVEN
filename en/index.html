DRAGON AGE UNIVERSE

An original story grounded in the Dragon Age games, official novels, comics and lore.

Create your hero. Decide how to act. Love. Explore Thedas - and face the consequences.

Do not just read. Live the story.

Your own hero

Choose a race, class, origin, gender, portrait and starting attributes. Every character follows their own journey.

Choices with consequences

Branching scenes, uneasy alliances, relationships, secrets, multiple endings and chances to return for missing evidence.

Explore Thedas

Dark fantasy, factions, archives, cities and legends connected to four eras of Dragon Age.

Every choice leaves a mark on your story

Your adventure is available in Ukrainian and English. Progress is saved on your device. With Google sign-in and cloud synchronization enabled, your progress can also be saved to Google Drive.
