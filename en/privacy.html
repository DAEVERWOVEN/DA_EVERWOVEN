Privacy Policy

How DA UNIVERSE handles Google account information and game progress.

Last updated November 14, 2026.

1. About the app and contact

DA UNIVERSE: The Ashen Atlas is an unofficial fan-made Android application. For privacy questions, contact dauniversebook@gmail.com.

2. Google information and permissions

For authentication, the app requests Google Sign-In and account identity information needed to identify your profile (Google account ID and email; Google may provide a profile name and image). For cloud saves, it requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This permission accesses only the hidden application data created by this app, not all your Google Drive files.

3. Character and progress data

Character names, origins, attributes, choices, relationships and story progress are stored on your device. When Google synchronization is used, the app copies these to its own da_universe_saves_v1.json file in your Google Drive hidden appDataFolder. The developer does not operate a separate central database of these saves.

4. Use and sharing

Google account data is used to sign in, select the correct profile and synchronize saves. The application contacts Google services for these functions. The developer does not sell Google account data to advertisers or use it for targeted advertising.

5. Website and third parties

This static website has no sign-in forms, analytics, advertising trackers or third-party font requests. The hosting provider may keep normal security and traffic logs under its own privacy practices. Google handles information during sign-in and storage under Google’s privacy practices.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until removed from your device. A cloud copy may remain in your Google Drive until deleted. Deleting a character in the app marks that character as deleted for synchronization; it does not necessarily erase the entire cloud save file. You can revoke app access in your Google Account settings. For help with data deletion, email the contact above.

7. Security and changes

The app uses authorization tokens to send requests to Google services. No online system can guarantee absolute security. We will update this policy if data practices change and publish the latest version here.
